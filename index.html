Overview¶

Lumi FDTD (finite difference time domain) powers full wave electromagnetic simulation and inverse design in photonics and RF. Free GPU cloud notebooks available on Lumi Colab. See our benchmark against Lumerical and Tidy3D in silicon photonics. Maintained by Luminescent AI, founded by a Stanford alum designing next generation photonic chips. Features¶

Performant

  • GPU accelerated up to 5000 MCells/s

  • Up to billion(s) cell domains

  • Adaptive mesh and tensor subpixel smoothing for accuracy and efficiency

AI design

  • Fully differentiable for inverse design

  • Topology optimization of multiple 2D or 3D structures

  • Length scale constrained for fabrication compliance

Comprehensive

  • Anisotropic, nonlinear, dispersive, metallic materials

  • PML, periodic, PEC, PMC boundaries

  • Adaptive mesh for thin or sharp geometries

  • Modal or field monitors in time or frequency domain

  • Embedded FDFD and FEM mode solver

Easy

  • Inverse design and simulation in Python frontend

  • .gds KLayout and gdsfactory integration

  • .stl / .step 3D geometry import

Coming soon

  • Bloch boundary

  • RF radiation patterns and far field projections
